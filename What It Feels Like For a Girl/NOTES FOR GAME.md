##### THE LAST UNICORN
* ==The Unicorn's meaning==;
in one way, she represents girlhood, in another, she represents God -- she also represents the union between the two
	"Godhood is just like girlhood; a begging to be believed", Kristin Chang
			girlhood, like Godhood, is always fleeting
			girlhood, like Godhood, is always on the cusp of disbelief
- ==The harpy & femininity==
*Amalthia*, representative of both innocent and maternal essence - embodies the Madonna archetype: maternal, yet pure - melancholy: pretty in suffering

*Harpy*, represents horror with bare breasts, untethered rage & unpredictable nature - rage: messier & more damaging to others
	"I will always be the virgin-prostitute, the perverse angel, the two-faced sinister and saintly woman", Anaïs Nin 
* ==The curse of mortality==
immense grief & the fear of passing of time - to be born is to, one day, die
	"[...] go from innocence in childhood, to sadness in adolescence, to bitterness in adulthood [...]"
*puberty*, becoming hyper aware of one's own body & the changes accuring
	- AFABS are confronted repeatedly with the passing of time due to the cyclical nature of the female body: "I was innocent and wise and full of pain, now that I'm a woman, everything is strange", The Last Unicorn
==Desire & Danger==
everyone, at one point, desires beauty. Many spend their entire lives longing for beauty - either possess it for oneself or in the form of another, such as sleeping with beautiful women or encaging all the unicorns of the world fro your own (and only) viewing pleasure
To be something so rare (temporary) & precious (vulnerable, naive) is to be desired - to be desired is to be hunted
	- "Being born a woman is an awful tragedy. My consuming desire to [sleep in an open field, to travel west, to walk freely at night], all is spoiled by the fact that I am a girl - a female, always in danger of assault and battery", Sylvia Plath
	- "My consuming interest in men & their lives is often misconstrued as a desire to seduce them, or as an invitation to intimacy", Sylvia Plath
==The price of regret==
Amalthia is a God wrapped in the flesh of a girl, & it drives her mad. To be a spirit so grand, so divine, confined to a much smaller space than she is accustomed (& belongs) to is horrifying.

##### I SAW THE TV GLOW, Gender as Body Horror
==The terror of body horror==
one thing that ties us to the horror, in this portrayal of the human body, is that we ALL have a boy we can touch, see. 
What is weird, what is unknown & what's going against the grain is what scares people, heavily defined by historical context (time period).  This shared fear brings people together off the basis of excluding & alienating the ones that they're scared of. 
* the main fear is influenced by who belongs to the "norm" group.
==Puberty as body horror==
the idea that your body can be manipulated into a form that you have no control over and all you can do is watch & accept that this idealized version of you is gone is a terrifying experience. 
* your physical vessel is a prison
		gender norms: being stuck by what society expects of you
