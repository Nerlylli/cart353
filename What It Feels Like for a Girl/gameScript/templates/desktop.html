My Diary






Dear diary, When I was younger, I wanted to live on the moon. I imagined myself becoming a princess astronaut as an adult, and traveling through the galaxy. I would be the first woman to ever step foot on every single planet in the Milky Way, and the first person to travel through other galaxies. I would live in space for the rest of my days, get old, explode in my rocket ship and become one with the stars.
Now the moon portends a difficult, unclear path ahead. What will be of tomorrow? Will I become everything I’ve ever dreamed of or just a physical proof of my wasted potential? Will I become like my mom?; end up with someone like dad?
I hate the moon, it only shows up when it’s dark outside. It leaves me all alone with my thoughts. If I ever become an astronaut, I’m not setting foot on that planet.
